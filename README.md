## Arb Station

Cross-exchange scanner for BTC, ZEC and SOL, plus a wallet-connected flash loan arbitrage tab.
Scan only by default. It holds no keys and every trade is approved in your own wallet.

## Deploy

Tap the button, sign in to Render with GitHub, review the service, and approve. Free plan, US region.

[![Deploy to Render](https://render.com/images/deploy-to-render-button.svg)](https://render.com/deploy)

When the service says Live, open its onrender.com address and press Start scanning.
The spider window shows whether the scanner is working.
