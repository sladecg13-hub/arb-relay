Reality check
On Ethereum mainnet, a public transaction like this is visible to MEV bots, which often copy or beat it. Use a private RPC such as Flashbots Protect in your wallet's network settings. Profit has to beat about 0.65% in swap fees plus the flash fee plus gas, and gaps that large between two big pools are rare and short. Expect this to find nothing most days. No profit is guaranteed.
